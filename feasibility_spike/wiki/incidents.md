# Incidents

Two of these are **genuine bugs** (they would have silently voided the
diversity grid). The third is a **debugging pitfall** we hit ourselves and
corrected. The reward and spawn changes are **not** in this file — they are
design decisions (`reward.md`, `decisions.md`), not bugs.

## Bug 1 — training updates scaled with diversity N

- **Symptom.** `train.py` created **one environment per track**, so `n_envs`
  equalled the diversity level. SB3 performs one gradient update per
  `vec_env.step()`, so under a fixed total step budget the number of updates
  scaled as `2M / n_envs`: **2,000,000 updates at 1 track but only 20,000 at
  100 tracks.** The high-diversity cells were starved of updates — the grid
  would have measured "fewer updates", not "more diversity".
- **Root cause.** Diversity was implemented as parallel envs instead of one
  env sampling from a pool.
- **Fix.** A **single environment** with a **stratified track-pool sampler**
  on `reset()`, so `n_envs` is always 1. Episodes truncate at **3,000 steps**
  so `reset()` fires and the pool rotates.
- **Verification.** Identical update counts at diversity 1 and 100.
- **Consequence for us.** `n_envs = 1` is now a pinned protocol constant —
  see `methodology.md`. Under a fixed budget, more envs = fewer updates, no
  extra data.

## Bug 2 — shared ray-caster across environments

- **Symptom.** `RaceCar.scan_simulator` is a **class attribute** in
  `f1tenth_gym`, shared **process-wide**. Every environment raycast against
  whichever map loaded **last**, so multi-track runs were effectively
  single-track runs — the diversity variable did nothing.
- **Root cause.** Found by reading the library source: the scan simulator is
  shared state, not per-instance.
- **Fix.** Removed by the single-environment fix (one env, so one map at a
  time, no cross-contamination).
- **Verification.** 20 of 20 tracks now visited, evenly dealt.

Together these two bugs are why the timeline slide says the grid would have
been "voided" — both silently collapse diversity to 1.

## Pitfall — VecNormalize loaded without flags → garbage observations

- **Symptom.** An early analysis claimed SAC "crashes at 2.7–10.7 m on a
  straight." Wrong.
- **Root cause.** `VecNormalize.load(path, venv)` (2-arg signature) does not
  by itself put the wrapper in eval mode. Without `training=False` and
  `norm_obs=True`, observations are un-normalized garbage → the policy
  receives off-distribution input → wrong actions → a fake crash pattern.
- **Fix.** Use the team's `load_vecnormalize` helper (or set the flags
  explicitly). The correct loader shows the real two-phase failure (creep
  ~0.6 m/s, then snap to full speed + hard-left-lock into the wall) at
  ~34 m / ~26 m — see `experiments.md`.
- **Lesson (wire-log the boundary).** When a policy behaves strangely, log
  the literal observation vector at the wrapper boundary **before**
  hypothesising about the policy. One `print(obs[:5])` would have caught the
  un-normalized values immediately.

## October 2026 — four measurement pitfalls

None of these are code bugs. Each produced a confident claim that was later
retracted, and each has a cheap check that would have caught it.

### Pitfall — same track names, different tracks (seed 0 vs seed 123)
- **Symptom.** "SAC cannot lap its own training tracks at any diversity" (2026-10-06).
- **Root cause.** Desmond's models were evaluated on locally installed `synthetic_track_*`
  generated by `make_synth_tracks.py --seed 0`. The canonical pool is seed 123
  (`generate_track_pool.py`). **0/20 match** (track 0: 164.06 m vs 189.04 m). The old wiki
  handoff gave the seed-0 command, and Desmond corrected it on 2026-10-05 (`handoff.md`, `log.md`).
- **Check that caught it.** Track length from a full-precision log, `progress_m / laps`,
  compared against the installed map.
- **Fix.** D12: install from the manifest; `train.py` and `evaluate.py --track-set` verify checksums.

### Pitfall — a training-log number quoted as performance
- **Symptom.** "The sweep helped PPO ~40%" was claimed from training-log distance at a partial budget.
- **Root cause.** No held-out evaluation behind it. The matched-checkpoint held-out evaluation gave
  +72% distance for the best variant only, and **0 laps for every variant** (`results.md`).
- **Fix.** Methodology section "Training-log vs evaluation"; D15.

### Pitfall — two variables changed, credit given to one
- **Symptom.** "γ is the lever; n_steps only amplifies it" and "exploration rose 9× from γ alone".
- **Root cause.** G999 was compared against V0, which differs in **both** γ and n_steps. The
  one-variable 2×2 shows an interaction: neither alone produces laps (`ppo-diagnosis.md`).
- **Fix.** Compare one variable at a time, at a matched step count.

### Pitfall — one checkpoint read as a trend
- **Symptom.** "The final checkpoint is worse than 800 k (40% → 0%)", and earlier, "λ 0.99 hurts".
- **Root cause.** Adjacent checkpoints swing 0 ↔ 10/25 held-out. Two points from a volatile
  curve make any story. λ 0.99 turned out to be slower, not worse (59% vs 65% at 2 M).
- **Fix.** D14: average the last 5 checkpoints; report the curve.

### Pitfall — `run_config.json` says `running` for killed runs
`train.py` writes `completed` only at the end, so killed runs (X1, R2–R4) still say `running`.
The real state is tabled in `results/ppo_diagnosis/README.md`.

### Note — training is not bit-reproducible, evaluation is
A re-run of Desmond's PPO d1 with the same seed differs from iteration 0, because training *samples*
from the policy and floating-point differences compound. Deterministic evaluation of his saved
weights reproduced to 0.0000 m. So "same seed" ≠ "same run": use ≥3 seeds.

## October 8–23: process pitfalls (none affected the final results)

### Pitfall — proposing a configuration without fetching the team's branches
- **Symptom.** On 2026-10-09 a "proposed final config" and a teammate zip were built without knowing that the team had
  already decided grid A on `origin/final-grid` (`7552398`, 10-08).
- **Cause.** Remote branches had not been fetched since 10-07.
- **Fix.** The zip README was rewritten to open with the team decision and options A/B/C (grid B came from option C).
  Lesson: `git fetch --all` and read every `DECISION*.md` before proposing.

### Pitfall — background runs reported as running after they had died
- **Symptom.** `G999_SAC` and `R1_repeat10_alone` were described as running until 10-09, but both had died on 10-06 ~22:05.
  The S1P5 SAC runs stopped at ~1.81M with no final checkpoint, which went unrecorded until 10-23.
- **Cause.** Status was read from `run_config.json` (which says `running` until the end) and from memory, not from the
  process list and log tails.
- **Fix.** Check the process list and the log tail before reporting status (see the run_config pitfall above).

### Pitfall — two width measures on one slide
- **Symptom.** A draft slide compared 1.06 m (synthetic) with 1.11 m (real), while slide 5 used 1.07 m for real.
- **Cause.** The verification JSONs' `median_wall_clearance_m` reads 3–6 cm higher than the geometry method behind
  `track_summary.csv`.
- **Fix.** Same method for both sets (1.02 / 1.07 m); decision D20.

### Pitfall — grid A's checkpoint selection committed after the test
- **Symptom.** `selection.csv` for grid A was written 10-09 19:06 but committed with the results (10-10 19:00).
- **Mitigation.** Recomputing the rule from the raw vw_val logs reproduces all 48 picks, and model sha256s tie picks to files.
  The deck's reflection slide lists "commit every selection before testing" as a lesson.

### Pitfall — agent-level statistics read as algorithm-level claims
- **Symptom.** Draft slides and the talk script said "PPO gains more from diversity in grid B" (p = 0.018), "the drop lowers
  every score" and "more tracks help on every seed".
- **Cause.** A track-level test of three agents per cell, driven by one seed (without seed 2 it reverses); 7/48 agents
  rise from synthetic to real; two grid-B PPO seeds got worse on real circuits.
- **Fix.** Report every seed; name the test family; agreed wording list (D21, `final-presentation.md`).
